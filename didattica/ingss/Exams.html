Final Exams: Rules and Dates

Rules

Final examinations will be based on:

  • A written exercise, where the students will be given 3 hours to analyse and design (using appropriate notations and conceptual tools) a software system based on a set of proposed requisites and characterisics. presentations based by students
  • An oral exam, following the written exercixe and conditioned to its success,and focusses on general discussion on any of the topics touched during the course.
  • The discussion, during the oral exam, of a specific topic/problem of interest to the student, in the form of a 15 minutes presentation of technical/scientific articles related to this topic/problem.
Alternatively, for those students regularly attending the course, it is possible to develop a group project (2-3 persons) of software engineering. Details to be discussed with the teacher.

Dates

The dates for the exams will be scheduled in due time, also accounting for possible conflict with other exams of the same year.

We plan to schedule two written exams during the summer session.
