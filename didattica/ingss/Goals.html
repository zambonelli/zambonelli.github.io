Objectives and Topics

Objectives

The course has the general goal of introducing the key principles and tools of sof software engineering, service engineering, and complex socio-technical systems engineering.

The specific sub-goals of the course can be summarized as follows:

  • To learn and practice with the basic principles of software engineering.
  • To learn and to practice with the basic principles and technologies for distributed service systems development.
  • To acquire the capability of planning and managing the development process of complex software and service systems.
  • To get acknoloedge with the characteristics of modern socio-technical systems intended as complex adaptive systems.
  • To propose complex adaptive systems and collective adaptation phenomena as tools for the design and management of complex service systems.

Topics

The lecture of the course can slightly vary from year to year. However, in general, the lectures will definitely touch the following topics:

Part I: Software Engineering and Software Processes

  • The peculiarities of software as a product
  • Critical aspects of software
  • The software process: phases and characteristics
  • Software process models (waterfall, spiral, evolutive, agile)
  • The RUP process model
  • Software metrics (quantitative and qualitative)
  • UML Modelling (use cases, class diagrams, interaction diagrams, state diagrams)
  • Managing the development process
  • COCOMO
  • CMMI
  • Security issues in software development

Part II: Service Engineering and Complex Adaptive Systems

  • Software as a service
  • An overview of middleware technologies
  • Service oriented architectures
  • Service oriented technologies
  • Process models for service-oriented systems
  • Modern service systems as complex adaptive systems
  • Social networks and social network services
  • Services as agents, agent-oriented models and multiagent systems
  • Collective intelligence models and services
  • Autonomic computing and pervasive service ecosystems
