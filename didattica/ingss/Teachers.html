About the Teachers

Prof. Franco Zambonelli

Franco Zambonelli is full professor in computer science since 2010. He got the Laurea in Electronic Engineering in 1992 and the PhD in Computer Science in 1997. His research interests include software engineering for distributed computing, multiagent systems engineering, pervasive computing and self-organization in distributed computing. In these areas, he has published more than 200 scientific articles, co-edited 11 books, and written 2 monographs. He is in the Editorial Board of the Journal of Pervasive and Mobile Computing, the Journal of Agent-oriented Software Engineering, the ACM Transactions on Autonomous and Adaptive Systems.

He meet students on Tuesday morning (9.30-12.00).

More information can be found in his Personal Home Page.
