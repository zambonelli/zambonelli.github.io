Software and Service Engineering

a Complex Adaptive Systems Perspective


by Franco Zambonelli and Andrea Sassi


This site includes all the necessary information and material for the course on "Software Engineering and Service Engineering". News, events, and changes to the schedule of the classes will be published on the Facebook group.

Please note that this is mostly a computer science course, focussing on the issues related to the analysis, design, and management of modern software and service systems. However, the course has been shaped by explicitly taking into account the specific needs of Management Engineering students.

Accordingly, more than focussing on specific software technologies, the course will prominently focus on (i) the issue of managing the software and services development process and (ii) the understanding of the peculiar characteristics of the socio-technical scenarios in which such systems will operate and (iii) the study of how the lessons of complex adaptive systems and collective adaptive systems ccan help with this regard.
