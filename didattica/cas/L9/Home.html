Readings

For the exam, you must read at least one of these, at your choice.

D. Watts, S. Strogatz, "Collective Dynamics of Small World Networks", Nature, Vol. 393, pp. 393-395, June 1998. PDF . This original paper re-opening interest in small worlds networks.

H. Jeong, A. Barabasi, "The Diameter of the World Wide Web", Nature, Vol. 401, pp. 130-131, September 1999. PDF . This paper firstly proved the Web is a small world network.

M. Newman, "Models of the Small World", Journal of Statistical Physics, Vol. 101, pp. 819-841, 2001. PDF . A very friendly introduction to small world theory.

M. Newman, D. Watts, "Scaling and Percolation in Small World Networks", Physical Review E, Vol. 60, pp. 7332-7342, 1999. PDF . Analyses several properties of small world networks and, specifically, of percolation.
