Complex Adaptive Systems
a Software Engineering Perspective

Course by Franco Zambonelli and Marco Mamei


This site includes all the necessary information and material for the course on "Complex Adaptive Systems".



Please note that this is mostly a software engineering course, focussing on the design and development of complex adaptive *software* systems. Clearly, the lessons of natural complex adaptive systems are very important in this course, but they are not considered per se, but only w.r.t. their application in distributed systems engineering.
