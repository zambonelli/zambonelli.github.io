Objectives of the Course

The course has the general goal of introducing the general concept related to the structure and dynamics of complex systems, and applying this concepts to the engineering and understanding of modern distributed systems.

Modern distributed systems, from P2P and world-wide networks, to pervasive computing systems and sensor networks, in fact, must (i) exhibit the capability of adapting their behaviour to the characterstics and the dynamics of the operational environment; (ii) defend from and offensively exploit the emergenge of complex behaviours. That is, these systems must learn from the lessons of natural complex adaptive systems.
