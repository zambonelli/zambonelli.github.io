Swarm Intelligence

General Introduction to Swarm Intelligence: self-organization in animal colonies, useful behavior from emergence and local interactions. Examples of swarm intelligence in nature and their applications. Firefly synchronization, ant sorting, ant foraging, termites nest building. The concept of stigmergy. Systems based on swarm intelligence: ant routing, anthill, swarmlinda. Conclusions and open issues.

Readings

For the exam, you must read at least one of these, at your choice.

V. Parunak, "Go to the Ants: Engineering Principles from Natural Agent Systems", Annals of Operations Research, Vol. 75, pp. 69-101, 1997. PDF A good and easy introduction to ant-based systems.

V. Parunak, S, Brueckner, "Entropy and Self-organization in Multiagent Systems", 1st International Conference on Autonomous Agents and Multiagent Systems, Bologna (I), July 2001. PDF An interesting discussion on how concepts of entropy and the second law of thermodynamic apply to swarm systems and, in general, to self-organizing systems

M. Dorigo, V. Maniezzo, A. Colorni, "The Ant System: Optimization by a Colony of Cooperating Systems", IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics, Vol. 236, No. 1, pp. 1-13, 1996. PDF This is mostly an operation research paper, but most of the presented concepts apply to distributed systems and, specifically, to routing algorithms.

O. Babaoglu, H. Meling, A. Montresor, "Anthill: a Framework for the Development of Agent-based P2P Systems", 22nd International Conference on Distributed Computing Systems, 2002. PDF An interesting application of swarm intelligence to P2P computing.
