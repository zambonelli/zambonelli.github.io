Readings

For the exam, you must read at least one of these, at your choice.

J. Bradshaw, "An Introduction to Software Agents", in Software Agents, edited by J. Bradshaw, AAAI Press, 1998. PDF

J. Odell, "Objects and Agents Compared", Journal of Object Technology, Vol. 1, No. 1, June 2002. PDF

M. Winikoff, "Simplifying the Development of Intelligens Agents", Technical Report Tr-01-03, RMIT University Australia, 2001. PDF

M. Mataric, "Situated Robotics", Enciclopedia of Cognitive Science, Nature Publishing Group, 2002. PDF Please note that although this article talks about robots, its architectural considerations can seamlessly applied to software agents.

In addition, you may wish to have a look at "An Interview with Patty Maes" , appeared in IEEE Internet Computing on 1997, where one of the inventor of agents provides its visions and perspectives.
