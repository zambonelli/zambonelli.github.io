Agent-Oriented Software Engineering

Agent-oriented computing as a paradigm for developing applications in modern scenarios. Key concepts of agent-oriented software engineering. Agent-oriented software engineering vs. Object-oriented software engineering. Agent-oriented methodologies. The Gaia methodology. A case study. Implementing agent systems. Intra-agent issues. Inter-agent issues. Multiagent infrastructures. Communication infrastructures vs. Coordination infrastructures. Jade and MARS. Conclusions and open issues.

Readings

For the exam, you must read at least one of these, at your choice.

F. Zambonelli, N. Jennings, M. Wooldridge, "Developing Multiagent Systems: the Gaia Methodology", ACM Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology, Vol. 17, No. 3, July 2003. PDF

F. Zambonelli, A. Omicini, "Challenges and Research Directions in Agent-oriented Software Engineering", Journal of Autonomous Agents and Multiagent Systems, Vol. 9, No. 3, July 2004. PDF

B. Horling, V. Lesser, "A Survey of Multiagent Organizational Paradigm", University of Massachussets Technical Reports, 2004-12, 2004. PDF

J. Lind, "Issues in Agent-oriented Software Engineering", 1st International Workshop on Agent-oriented Software Engineering, Limerick (IR), June 2000. PDF

An additional "funny" paper you may wish to look at is: "The Agents' Hell: a Scenario of Worst Practices in Agent-oriented Software Engineering" , by F. Zambonelli and M. Luck, appeared in IEEE Computer on April 2004.
