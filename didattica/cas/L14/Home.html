Mobile Robots and Self-Assembly

Mobile robots and self-assembly. State of the art, applications, and visions. Algorithms for mobile robot coordination. Field-based approaches to robot cooperative motion. Impact of the capabilities of robots. Self-assembly as a specific form of mobile robotics. The amorphous computing approach. Other approaches. Limitations. Modular Robots.

Readings

For the exam, you must read at least one of these, at your choice.

W.-M. Shen, B. Salemi, P. Will, "Hormone-Inspired Adaptive Communicatissson and Distributed Control for CONRO Self-Reconfigurable Robots", IEEE Transactions on Robotcs and Automation, Vol. 15, No. 8, October 2002. PDF Description of the CONRO modular robots.

W.-M. Shen, P. Will, A. Galstyan, C. Chuong, "Hormone-Inspired Self-organization and Distributed Control of Robotics Swarm", Autonomous Robots, Vol. 17, pp. 93-105, 2004. PDF An interesting approach to field-based coordination of large robot swarms.

M. Mamei, M. Vasirani, F. Zambonelli, "Experiments of Morphogenesis in Swarms of Simple Robots", Journal of Applied Artificial Intelligence, Vol. 18, No. 10, December 2004. PDF An easy paper about using fields for self-assembly of simple particles-robots.

R. Nagpal, "Programmable Self-Assembly Using Biologically-Inspired Multiagent Control", 1st International Joint Conference on Autonomous Agents and Multiagent Systems, Bologna (I), July 2002. PDF The MIT Approach to self-assembly via sort of field-based coordination.
