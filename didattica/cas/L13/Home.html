Field-based Coordination

Field-based coordination as a specific types of stigmergic coordination. Examples of Field-based swarm intelligence phenomena: flocking, prey-predators, morphogenesis. Modeling field-based coordination in dynamical systems terms. Field-based coordination for pervasive computing: location-dependent computing, motion coordination. Programming field-based coordination: the TOTA middleware. Conclusions and open issues.

Readings

For the exam, you must read at least one of these, at your choice.

M. Mamei, F. Zambonelli, "Co-Fields: a Physically Inspired Approach to Motion Coordination", IEEE Pervasive Computing, Vol. 4, No. 2, April 2004. PDF An easy paper about using co-fields in distributed motion coordination.

M. Mamei, F. Zambonelli, "Programming Pervasive and Mobile Computing Applications with the TOTA Middleware", 2nd International Conference on Pervasive Computing and Communication, Orlando (FL), March 2004. PDF The basic paper about field-based programming in TOTA.

G. C. Roman, C. Julien, Q. Huang, "Network Abstractions for Context-Aware Mobile Computing", 24th International Conference on Software Engineering, May 2002. PDF An interesting context-aware field model.

S. Bandini, S. Manzoni, C. Simone, "Dealing with Space in Situated Multiagent Systems", 1st International Conference on Autonomous Agents and Multiagent Systems, Bologna (I), July 2002. PDF An interesting general model of field-based coordination.
