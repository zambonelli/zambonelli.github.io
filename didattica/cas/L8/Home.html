Readings

For the exam, you must read at least one of these, at your choice.

S. Wolfram, "Cellular Automata as Models of Complexity", Nature, Vol. 311, pp. 419-424, October 1984. On Line Version . This is the first paper that clearly identified the properties of CA as complex systems.

F. Zambonelli, A. Roli, M. Mamei, "What Can Cellular Automata Tell Us About the Behaviour of Large Scale Multiagent Systems", in Software Engineering for Large Scale Agent Systems, LNCS, 2004. PDF . To get a clue of why CA may be important for the study of modern distributed systems.

P. Sarkhar, "A Brief History of Cellular Automata", ACM Computing Surveys, Vol. 32, No. 1, March 2000. PDF . A General and Complete Survey of CA.

N. Ganghuly, "A Survey on Cellular Automata", The BISON Project Technical Report, 2004. PDF . A general overview of approaches and problems.
