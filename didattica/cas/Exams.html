Rules for the Exams (Year 2010)

Examinations will be based on presentations based by students at the end of the course, where each students is assigned a specific topic to study (typically, this consists in analysing 3-4 scientific papers on a specific topic and in the implementation of a simple prototype/simulation related to that topic), and to report on it via a 30-minutes presentation followed by an open discussion in which the presentation will be put in relations with other topics of the course.

Following the presentation, an oral examination on the various topics touched during the course will further assess the knowledge of the students.

The final evaluation will be based on the quality of the presentated synthesis, of the protptype/simulation realized, on the capability of the students to put this in general relations with the topic of complex adaptive systems, other than on their knowledge of the various topics of the course.
