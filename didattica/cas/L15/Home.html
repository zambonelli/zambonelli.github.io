The Cloak of Invisibility: Towards the Spray Computers Vision

What is the spray computers vision. Direct vs. Reverse engineering approaches to self-organization. The invisible wall. The invisible object. The cloak of invisibility.

Readings

This lecture if mostly based on the following paper:

F. Zambonelli, M. Mamei, "The Cloak of Invisibility: Challenges and Applications", IEEE Pervasive Computing, Vol. 1, No. 4, October 2002. PDF.

But the spray computer paper also has something related to it:

F. Zambonelli, M.P. Gleizes, M. Mamei, R. Tolksdorf, "Spray Computers: Explorations in Self-Organization", Journal of Pervasive and Mobile Computing, Vol. 1, No. 1, March 2005. PDF.

Interesting Links

The Optical Camouflage Project at the University of Tokyo. A totally different approach to invisibility, based on optical manipulations and videocamera. Still, very interesting (and implemented, indeed!).

Invisibility via Plasmodic Material. A scientific news from the Universisity of Pennsilvanya, wnere scientist have discovered a material that can "absorb" light and become virtually invisible. Interesting how not only technology but also material science can contribute to the vision.

PLEASE NOTE: When searching for invisibility on Google or other search engines, you will mostly get results on "social invisibility". A few of the results matches tecnological or scientific means to achieve invisibility...
